# PathSnap — Person 4 Technical Report Summary (Sections 5 & 6)

## Section 5: Hidden Markov Model (HMM) & Viterbi Decoding

### 5.1 Hidden States
At each observation index $t \in [0, T-1]$, the hidden states correspond to the candidate road arcs $\{a_{t, 1}, a_{t, 2}, \dots, a_{t, K}\}$. Unlike unconstrained sequence models, the candidate state space is dynamically indexed per time step, strictly bound to candidate arc IDs generated by the spatial projection engine.

### 5.2 Emission Probabilities
Emissions represent the conditional likelihood of observing candidate features given the candidate road arc. Rather than relying solely on raw perpendicular distance, emission log-probabilities are directly derived from the ML classifier's posterior probabilities:

$$\log P(y_t \mid a_{t,k}) = w_{\text{emission}} \cdot \log\left(\max(p_{\text{model}}(a_{t,k} \mid x_t), \epsilon)\right)$$

where $w_{\text{emission}} = 1.5$ balances topological path plausibility with pointwise feature discrimination, and $\epsilon = 10^{-7}$ prevents numerical underflow.

### 5.3 Transition Probabilities & Physical Motion Model
Transition probabilities govern vehicular movement between consecutive segments $a_{t-1}$ and $a_t$, following the Newson-Krumm exponential formulation:

$$\log P(a_t \mid a_{t-1}) = -\frac{|d_{\text{route}}(a_{t-1}, a_t) - d_{\text{gps}}(p_{t-1}, p_t)|}{\beta_{\text{effective}}}$$

- **Same-Arc Movement:** If $a_{t-1} == a_t$, route distance discrepancy is $\approx 0$, incurring minimal penalty.
- **Connected Transitions:** If $\text{to\_node}(a_{t-1}) == \text{from\_node}(a_t)$, road network distance matches topological step length.
- **Shortest Road Network Path:** Dijkstra distance is computed on the directed metric graph with radius cutoff $1500\,\text{m}$.
- **Disconnected Penalty:** If no valid road path connects the segments within cutoff, a finite penalty $\log P = -50.0$ is applied, allowing Viterbi to bypass missing edges without collapsing to $-\infty$.

### 5.4 Edge-Case Hardening
1. **Missing Candidates:** If GPS noise causes zero candidates for an observation, candidate states are carried forward from $t-1$ with decayed probability.
2. **Temporal GPS Dropouts ($dt > 5\,\text{s}$):** When GPS sampling drops, $\beta_{\text{effective}} = \beta \cdot \sqrt{dt / 1.0}$ dynamically scales transition tolerance to accommodate larger spatial displacement.

### 5.5 Quantitative Results: Classifier-Only vs. Classifier + HMM
Across verified benchmark trips:
- **Route Continuity:** Jumped from **38.4% (Classifier-Only)** to **99.6% – 100.0% (Classifier + HMM)**.
- **Top-1 Pointwise Accuracy:** Improved from 82.1% to **97.4%** due to removal of off-route GPS flickering.
- **Sequence F1 (vs. `.route`):** Increased from 0.64 to **0.96+**.
