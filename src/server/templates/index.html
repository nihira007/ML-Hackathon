HMM Formulation & Mechanics

  • Emission Probabilities: Calibrated probabilities output by the Optuna-tuned XGBoost model for candidate arcs within 50m of each GPS fix.
  • Transition Probabilities: Dijkstra shortest network distance exponentially penalized against Euclidean GPS displacement: P(a_t | a_{t-1}) ∝ exp(-|d_network - d_gps| / β) with β=25.0m.
  • Large GPS Gap & Dropout Handling: When consecutive fixes exceed 100m or 15s, transitions transition smoothly to topological shortest-path fallback, preventing disconnected trajectories.
  • Disconnected Segment Resolution: Viterbi trellis dynamic programming enforces strictly directed node-arc continuity, bridging non-adjacent observations through intermediate topological graph arcs.
